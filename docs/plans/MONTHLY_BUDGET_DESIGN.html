Monthly budget: This Month grows into the plan

Design mock for the monthly budget, the next step after expected expenses. All figures are illustrative and dated Nov 14 (day 14 of 30). The bills match the demo data; income assumes two $4,800 paychecks and a $500 savings goal. The “Can I afford it?” screen works: type an amount or pick a preset.

How the numbers work

Income expected (recurring inflows, like bills)
− Big bills (expected expenses, shipped)
− Savings goal (you set it)
= Everyday budget

Everyday budget − category plans = Splurge room
Everyday budget − spent so far = Left this month

Pace = plan × days gone ÷ days in month.

Defaults, so it works on day one

• Each category's plan starts at its median month over the last 3, leaving out the big bills.
• Income = recurring inflows detected like bills (payroll).
• Savings goal starts at $0 until you set it.
• Everything not planned falls into 'Everything else'.

Open questions

1. Calendar month or pay cycle?
2. Carry unspent plan into next month?
3. Credit-card spending counts when charged (not when the card is paid) — confirm.
4. Should splurge-check purchases be saved as planned, so the room shrinks?
5. Income that hasn't landed yet: count it, or only what's in?
